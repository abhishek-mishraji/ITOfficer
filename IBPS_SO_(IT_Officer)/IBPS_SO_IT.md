# IBPS SO (IT Officer) Complete Guide

## 📌 Overview

**IBPS SO (Specialist Officer - IT Officer)** is a national-level exam conducted by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) to recruit IT Officers in public sector banks in India.

- Conducting Body: IBPS
- Post: IT Officer (Scale I)
- Level: National
- Frequency: Once a year

## 🆕 Latest Published Cycle

**CRP-SPL-XV** is the most recently published Specialist Officer cycle visible on the official IBPS site.

- Window notification: 30 Jun 2025
- Detailed notification: 01 Jul 2025
- Prelims exam date: 30 Aug 2025
- Final result and final cutoff update: 15 Jan 2026
- Application reprint update: 09 Apr 2026

> Note: IBPS releases cutoff PDFs stage-wise and category-wise after the exam stages. Always verify the latest official PDF before relying on any cutoff value.

## 🎯 Eligibility Criteria

### 1. Nationality

- Indian citizen

---

### 2. Age Limit

- Minimum: 20 years
- Maximum: 30 years

**Age Relaxation:**

- SC/ST: +5 years
- OBC: +3 years
- PwBD: +10 years

---

### 3. Educational Qualification

**Required Degree:**

- B.Tech / B.E / M.Tech / MCA in:
  - Computer Science
  - Information Technology
  - Electronics & Communication

OR

- Graduate with relevant certifications in IT

## 📝 Exam Pattern

### 🔹 Preliminary Exam

| Section               | Questions | Marks | Time   |
| --------------------- | --------- | ----- | ------ |
| English Language      | 50        | 25    | 40 min |
| Reasoning Ability     | 50        | 50    | 40 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 50        | 50    | 40 min |

**Total:**

- Questions: 150
- Marks: 125
- Duration: 2 hours

---

### 🔹 Main Exam (IT Officer)

| Section                     | Questions | Marks | Time   |
| --------------------------- | --------- | ----- | ------ |
| Professional Knowledge (IT) | 60        | 60    | 45 min |

---

### 🔹 Interview

- Total Marks: 100
- Minimum Qualifying:
  - General: 40%
  - SC/ST/OBC/PwBD: 35%
