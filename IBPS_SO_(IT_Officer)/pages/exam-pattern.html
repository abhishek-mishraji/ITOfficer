Exam pattern

Selection stages and marking scheme

The IBPS SO IT Officer route has a clear three-stage process. For this post, professional knowledge is the most important scoring area because the mains paper is heavily technical.

Selection flow

Prelims → Mains → InterviewEach stage has its own cut-off and shortlisting logic.

Stages

How the exam is structured

Stage 1Preliminary exam

English, reasoning, and quantitative aptitude.

Stage 2Main exam

Professional Knowledge (IT) paper with the highest importance.

Stage 3Interview

Final assessment for shortlisted candidates with category-wise qualifying marks.

Preliminary exam

SectionQuestionsMarksTime
English Language502540 min

Quantitative Aptitude505040 min

Total: 150 questions, 125 marks, 2 hours

Main exam

SectionQuestionsMarksTime
Professional Knowledge (IT)606045 min

The main paper is the decisive stage for IT graduates.

Interview

Total marks: 100

  • General: 40%
  • SC/ST/OBC/PwBD: 35%

Final merit is built from the published stage-wise rules.

Marking scheme

+1 for every correct answer, -0.25 for every wrong answer, and no penalty for unattempted questions.

Practical advice

Prepare for sectional speed in prelims, but spend most of your effort on technical accuracy for mains.

Mode of exam

How each stage is conducted

Prelims mode

Online computer-based test (CBT), objective type (MCQ).

Mains mode

Online computer-based test (CBT), objective type for Professional Knowledge.

Interview mode

Face-to-face interview round (not an MCQ test), conducted after mains shortlisting.
