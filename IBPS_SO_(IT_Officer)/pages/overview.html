Overview

IBPS SO IT Officer at a glance

This exam is conducted by IBPS for recruitment of Specialist Officer Scale I in participating public sector banks. It is a national-level test and the IT Officer stream is the most technical of the specialist posts.

Conducting body: IBPS Level: National Frequency: Once a year

Selection flow

Prelims → Mains → Interview Shortlisting is based on stage-wise performance and the final merit list is prepared from the published rules.

Key facts

What students should know first

Participating banks

Bank of Baroda, Bank of India, Canara Bank, Central Bank of India, Indian Bank, Indian Overseas Bank, Punjab National Bank, UCO Bank, Union Bank of India, and others as notified.

Role focus

An IT-oriented banking post that values core computer science knowledge, technical reasoning, and awareness of digital banking ecosystems.

Typical vacancy cycle

The official notification usually appears once the annual CRP specialist officer cycle is opened by IBPS.
