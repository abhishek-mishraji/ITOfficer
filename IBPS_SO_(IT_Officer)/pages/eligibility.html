Eligibility

Who can apply

Check the core eligibility requirements before starting the application. Always confirm the final details in the latest official notification because IBPS may update age or qualification rules in a new cycle.

Quick snapshot

Nationality, age, and degree matter mostThe IT Officer stream typically expects an engineering or IT-related background.
01

Nationality

Applicant must be an Indian citizen.

02

Age limit

Minimum 20 years and maximum 30 years, with relaxations for reserved categories.

  • SC/ST: +5 years
  • OBC: +3 years
  • PwBD: +10 years
03

Educational qualification

B.Tech, B.E, M.Tech, or MCA in Computer Science, Information Technology, or Electronics & Communication.

Some notifications also accept candidates with relevant IT certifications or equivalent qualifications.
