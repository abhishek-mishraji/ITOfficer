How To Use

7-day PYQ cycle for fast improvement

Day 1 to 2

Attempt full paper

Take one prelims-style and one IT mini-paper in exam timing conditions.

Day 3 to 5

Error log and drills

Classify mistakes into concept, speed, and question reading errors; run targeted drills.

Day 6 to 7

Re-attempt and review

Re-solve missed questions and one fresh paper; compare accuracy and attempt rate.

Tip: Prioritize accuracy first. For IT mains, a well-revised concept base often beats random high-volume question practice.
