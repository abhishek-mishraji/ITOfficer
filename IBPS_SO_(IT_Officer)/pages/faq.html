General category

Minimum needed to move to next stage

These are the latest usable benchmarks for IT Officer. Final values can change each cycle, so always confirm in the official IBPS cutoff PDF.

Prelims (General / UR)

  • English sectional minimum: 10.25 / 25
  • Reasoning sectional minimum: 6.50 / 50
  • Quant/GA sectional minimum: 9.50 / 50
  • Overall cutoff (IT Officer): 58.25 / 125

You must clear both sectional and overall cutoff to go to mains.

Mains + Interview (General / UR)

  • Mains Professional Knowledge minimum: 17.50 / 60
  • Mains category cutoff (UR trend): 23.25 / 60
  • Interview qualifying minimum (General): 40 / 100

You must clear mains cutoff and interview qualifying marks to be considered for final allotment.

Final merit logic: Prelims marks are generally qualifying in nature and are not added in final merit. Final merit is prepared from mains + interview scores after IBPS normalization. Commonly used weight split is mains 80% and interview 20%.
What is the biggest scoring area?

Professional Knowledge in the mains exam. For IT Officer aspirants, technical concepts matter more than generic aptitude after prelims.

When should I start preparing?

Start with the core CS subjects and aptitude basics as early as possible. A structured three-phase plan works best.

Are the cutoff numbers fixed every year?

No. Cutoffs vary with vacancies, difficulty level, and category-wise competition, so recent official trends are the best guide.

Is the final selection only based on mains?

Not only mains. Final merit is based on mains plus interview as per official scoring rules for that cycle.

What should I revise daily?

SQL, networking basics, DBMS, OS, and quick reasoning/quant practice. Daily revision is more useful than occasional long study sessions.

Can I use this page as a checklist?

Yes. The site is arranged to make it easy to move from overview to eligibility, then pattern, syllabus, cutoff, and preparation.
