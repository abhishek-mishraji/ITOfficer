How to interpret cutoffs

  • Prelims cutoffs are usually released post-wise and category-wise.
  • Mains cutoff for IT Officer is tied to Professional Knowledge.
  • Final selection depends on the combined score after interview.

Trends move with vacancy count, exam difficulty, and candidate volume.

Important note

IBPS does not always present every historical cutoff in a single easy-to-read table, so this guide highlights the most useful current trend data rather than guessing missing values.

Best practice

Use the latest official PDF for final cutoff confirmation and keep your mock-test target safely above the recent trend.
