Preparation

Study plan, books, and resources

A good plan for this exam starts with basics, then shifts to practice, and finishes with revision and mock tests. The goal is to build speed in prelims and accuracy in IT topics for mains.

Main rule

Practice daily, revise weeklyStudents who combine concept revision with question practice usually perform better in the technical section.
Phase 1

Basics for 1 to 2 months

Study core CS subjects and do daily aptitude practice.

Phase 2

Practice for 2 to 3 months

Attempt mock tests and previous year questions regularly.

Phase 3

Revision in the last month

Focus on weak areas, revise notes, and take daily mocks.
