Syllabus

What to study for prelims and mains

The prelims paper tests basic aptitude and language skills, while the mains paper is centered on technical professional knowledge. For the IT Officer stream, the mains syllabus matters most.

Focus area

Professional Knowledge carries the most weightCore CS subjects and practical banking technology concepts should be revised together.
